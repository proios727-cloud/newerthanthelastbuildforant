## Every entry by failure mode

### Wears out early (21)

- **Vacuum brush rolls** (cleaning, opp. 48): Hair and thread wind tightly around the brush roll and its ends until it stops spinning, so it has to be cut free with scissors
- **Social platforms (Instagram)** (creator-tools, opp. 36): Instagram keeps reweighting toward Reels and video, so artists' still-image posts and carousels reach a shrinking, unpredictable share of their own followers.
- **Mattresses** (bedroom, opp. 31): Foam mattresses sag into body impressions within a few years, and warranty claims fail on high depth thresholds
- **Shower caddies** (bathroom, opp. 30): Suction cups let go on textured tile and the caddy crashes down; wire caddies sold as rust-resistant rust within months
- **Pillows** (bedroom, opp. 30): Fiberfill pillows come out of the wash lumpy and flat even when the care tag is followed, so many people stop washing them
- **Cordless stick vacuums** (cleaning, opp. 30): The battery is built in, runs far short of the box claim on max power, and when it wears out replacing the whole vacuum is cheaper
- **Basket-style air fryers** (kitchen, opp. 30): Mesh and crevices trap grease; the basket is hard to fully clean
- **Garden hoses** (outdoor, opp. 30): Lightweight expandable hoses burst, split or leak at the connector within a season, and buyers return two or three in a row
- **Nonstick pans & air-fryer baskets** (kitchen, opp. 29): Coating flakes within weeks or months, sometimes after one wash; users worry about eating the flakes
- **Bathroom exhaust fans** (bathroom, opp. 23): Older fans get loud (often around 4 sones), and parts loosen and rattle with age, so people avoid running them
- **Dressers** (bedroom, opp. 23): Thin particle-board or cardboard drawer bottoms bow and fall out, and runners stick or let drawers drop onto each other
- **Mops & mop buckets** (cleaning, opp. 23): Wringers leave the mop dripping or don't fit the bucket, pads fray or catch, and buckets tip and spill dirty water
- **Extension cords** (garage, opp. 23): Long cords twist and kink into a tangle when coiled, and the kinks damage the wire inside
- **Food storage containers** (kitchen, opp. 23): Locking tabs snap off and lids stop sealing or don't fit
- **Patio cushions** (outdoor, opp. 23): Cushions fade within weeks to a year and grow mildew when left out, so they have to be carried inside after every rain
- **Shower curtain liners** (bathroom, opp. 21): Pink slime and mildew grow in the damp folds of the liner, which then has to be washed or thrown away
- **Fan CRM / direct-to-fan platforms** (creator-tools, opp. 21): Artists cannot reach their own fans: Spotify keeps listener contact data (criticized by M. Shadows), and platforms artists depend on for fan relationships wear out (Bandcamp lost about half its staff after the Songtradr sale and had more engineering layoffs in 2026; Bandsintown fan alerts have had outages).
- **Toilet paper holders** (bathroom, opp. 19): Swapping the roll on a spring-loaded rod takes two hands, and the spring fails or the arm falls off
- **Pegboard hooks** (garage, opp. 19): Thin 1/8-inch hooks wobble in 1/4-inch holes and fall out every time a tool is taken down
- **Memberships (Patreon iOS)** (creator-tools, opp. 17): Apple's 30% in-app fee and Patreon's repeatedly reversed billing deadlines (three policy reversals in 18 months) keep forcing creators to re-plan pricing and migrate their billing.
- **Resealable zipper bags** (packaging, opp. 15): The zipper tracks don't line up or come apart, and the easy-open tab tears off, so the bag never truly seals

### One size fits all (15)

- **Fitted sheets** (bedroom, opp. 48): Corners pop off overnight because pocket depths aren't standardized, the elastic is only at the corners, and toppers or adjustable bases add height or bend the mattress
- **Duvets & duvet covers** (bedroom, opp. 38): The insert slides and bunches inside the cover overnight, because nothing holds it in place and insert and cover sizes don't match
- **Streaming income** (creator-tools, opp. 30): Since 2024, Spotify tracks under 1,000 streams in 12 months earn no recording royalties at all, which hits long-tail and niche catalogs (an estimated $47M in 2024) and is now the subject of an artist lawsuit.
- **Child-resistant pill bottles** (packaging, opp. 30): Push-and-twist caps are painful or impossible for people with arthritis, leading to missed doses or bottles left open
- **Mattresses** (bedroom, opp. 23): Memory foam traps body heat and sleepers wake up sweaty, even on models marketed with cooling gel
- **Robot vacuums** (cleaning, opp. 23): The robot snags on socks, cords and rug tassels or wedges under low furniture, so owners tidy the floor before every run
- **Collaboration with musicians (album art, Spotify Canvas visuals)** (creator-tools, opp. 23): Cover and Canvas artists often go uncredited because streaming platforms have no field for visual artists, and some report being paid late or not at all after their art is already in promotion.
- **Bedding in washers & dryers** (laundry, opp. 23): Fitted sheets and duvet covers wad into a damp ball that swallows socks and small items and comes out wet in the middle
- **Kitchen lighting** (kitchen, opp. 22): Ceiling lights sit behind the cook and throw shadows onto the worktop
- **Duvets & duvet covers** (bedroom, opp. 21): Getting the insert into the cover is a two-person job unless you know the 'burrito' trick, because both far corners have to be held at once
- **Shower curtain liners** (bathroom, opp. 19): The liner billows inward and sticks to the person showering
- **Cordless stick vacuums** (cleaning, opp. 19): The trigger has to be held down the whole time and can't be locked on, which makes hands sore during longer cleans
- **Low-flow toilets** (bathroom, opp. 15): Older low-flow models with narrow trapways clog and need double flushing (much less common with newer models)
- **Shampoo & conditioner bottles** (bathroom, opp. 14): The two bottles are nearly identical and labeled in tiny type, so without glasses in a dim shower people can't tell them apart
- **French-door refrigerators** (kitchen, opp. 11): Water and icemaker lines run through usable space, freeze, or take up shelf room

### No feedback (14)

- **Streaming income transparency** (creator-tools, opp. 38): Stream counts in Spotify for Artists never line up with distributor royalty statements, which arrive 2-4 months late, so artists cannot tell if they are being paid correctly; one forum thread is titled 'Warning: Discrepancies in Stream Counts and Revenue'.
- **Grill propane tanks** (outdoor, opp. 38): Tanks have no fuel gauge, so the grill runs out mid-cook; add-on gauges are unreliable or restrict gas flow
- **Playlist pitching (Spotify for Artists)** (creator-tools, opp. 32): Artists pitch to Spotify editorial and hear nothing: no confirmation it was heard, no rejection, and the only signal is not getting placed.
- **Kitchen sponges** (cleaning, opp. 32): Sponges start to smell and harbor bacteria within days, yet most people keep them for a month or more because nothing shows when one is spent
- **Open calls / juried shows / grants (CaFÉ, Submittable, entry-fee calls)** (creator-tools, opp. 31): Artists pay entry fees for calls that accept 2-8% of applicants and hear nothing back except a silent rejection or a press release, so it feels like throwing money into a black hole.
- **Bathroom exhaust fans** (bathroom, opp. 30): The fan sounds like it's working but doesn't remove moisture (it's undersized, has a clogged grille, or is switched off too soon), which leads to mold and peeling paint
- **Clothes dryers** (laundry, opp. 29): Lint gets past the filter and builds up behind the drum or in the vent, where owners can't see or reach it, until it starts a fire
- **Recycling labels** (packaging, opp. 29): Recycling symbols are misread (the Green Dot is taken to mean recyclable), so many people guess or put packaging in the general waste
- **Music distributors** (creator-tools, opp. 26): Distributors (DistroKid and others) pull releases and freeze royalties over 'artificial streams' the artist says they never bought, give no evidence or strike warning, and sometimes say the decision cannot be appealed, while Spotify support sends artists back to the distributor.
- **Portfolio communities (Behance)** (creator-tools, opp. 23): Behance artists report shadow bans for 'suspicious activity' with no explanation, unclear curation and Pro lead distribution, and view analytics locked behind a paid tier.
- **Clothing care labels** (laundry, opp. 23): More than three dozen care symbols that most people can't read, so clothes get shrunk or ruined in the wash
- **Garage door openers** (garage, opp. 22): Nothing tells you the door was left open overnight, and built-in alerts are missing, fixed at an hour, or don't fire
- **Garage door keypads & remotes** (garage, opp. 19): Keypads and remotes stop working with no warning, usually a dead battery, lost pairing or lock mode, and each brand resets differently
- **Commission marketplaces (VGen)** (creator-tools, opp. 11): VGen's dispute process is opaque: when a commission goes wrong, support's decision can't be predicted or appealed, and it largely defers to each artist's own terms, so both buyers and artists feel unprotected.

### Seller-first (14)

- **Plastic wrap / cling film** (kitchen, opp. 48): The serrated cutter loses the film's edge, the wrap clings to itself, and the blade cuts fingers
- **Online-order shipping boxes** (packaging, opp. 41): Tiny items arrive in huge boxes stuffed with filler, wasting cardboard and space for recycling
- **Chip bags & snack boxes** (packaging, opp. 41): Bags and boxes are half empty, so buyers feel they're paying for air, especially after shrinkflation cuts
- **Print-on-demand shops (Redbubble, Teespring, bot shops)** (creator-tools, opp. 38): Bots and resellers lift artists' work onto print-on-demand shirts, and under the DMCA the artist has to find and report every listing one by one, often only to see it re-uploaded.
- **String trimmers** (outdoor, opp. 38): Overpriced proprietary spools jam or weld the line to itself, so the head has to be opened and rewound mid-job
- **Clamshell blister packs** (packaging, opp. 38): Sealed plastic clamshells need scissors or a knife to open, and opening them sends thousands of people a year to the emergency room with cuts
- **Online marketplaces (Etsy Offsite Ads)** (creator-tools, opp. 30): Etsy automatically enrolls sellers in Offsite Ads and takes a 12-15% cut of attributed orders on top of other fees, and once a shop crosses $10k in sales it can never opt out again.
- **Liquid detergent caps** (laundry, opp. 26): Faint or confusing fill lines inside the cap lead people to use too much detergent, which wastes money and leaves residue on clothes and in the machine
- **Music distributors** (creator-tools, opp. 23): With DistroKid the catalog goes offline if the annual subscription lapses unless the artist paid a per-release 'Leave a Legacy' fee ($29/$49), and the cheap headline price grows quickly with add-ons (Content ID, Store Maximizer, a Musician Plus upgrade just to set release dates).
- **Peel-off lids (yogurt, tins)** (kitchen, opp. 21): The tab tears off or the foil rips partway, and food splashes or the user gets cut
- **Snack & chip bags** (kitchen, opp. 21): Once opened, the bag can't be resealed and the food goes stale
- **Cereal box liners** (packaging, opp. 21): The inner bag rips unevenly down the side when opened, spills cereal into the box, and can't be reclosed
- **Cordless power tool batteries** (garage, opp. 20): Every brand, and sometimes each line within a brand, uses its own battery, so switching brands means buying new batteries and chargers
- **Paid curator submission (Groover, SubmitHub)** (creator-tools, opp. 16): Artists pay per submission and get copy-paste declines (one review: $150 bought 16 of 50 generic replies and 7 no-shows), free-credit declines come with no explanation, and credits for unanswered submissions are refunded inconsistently.

### Last-mile waste (8)

- **Commission / client work management (invoicing, contracts)** (creator-tools, opp. 48): Clients vanish once the art is delivered and the invoice goes out, and small extra requests pile up into unpaid scope creep because nothing records what was agreed.
- **Front-load washers** (laundry, opp. 36): Water and detergent sit in the door gasket folds after every load, growing mold and a musty smell that gets into clean clothes
- **Print-on-demand shops (Redbubble)** (creator-tools, opp. 30): Redbubble's tiered account fees eat a large share of small artists' earnings, and the $20 payout threshold keeps whatever is left stuck in the account.
- **Toothbrush holders** (bathroom, opp. 26): Water and toothpaste pool in the closed bottom of cup-style holders, turning into slime that is hard to reach and clean
- **Trigger spray bottles** (cleaning, opp. 26): Nozzles clog with dried cleaner, seals wear so the trigger sputters, and the last inch can't be sprayed because the tube no longer reaches
- **Publishing admin / royalty collection** (creator-tools, opp. 23): Self-published songwriters who only joined a PRO never collect streaming mechanicals; their unmatched royalties sit in a pot estimated at $1.5-2.5B, which can pass to the MLC after three years.
- **Marketplace / tip-jar payments (Etsy, Ko-fi via PayPal)** (creator-tools, opp. 19): Etsy payment reserves and PayPal holds on Ko-fi commissions lock up a share of an artist's sales for up to 21-45 days, often with no clear reason given and sometimes by mistake.
- **Music distributors** (creator-tools, opp. 19): Artists on TuneCore and UnitedMasters report withdrawal requests rejected and balances moved to 'funds on hold' or the account closed for unspecified 'abnormal activity', with payouts left pending for months.

### Bad timing (7)

- **Taxes / admin (1099-K, multi-platform income)** (creator-tools, opp. 30): Artists who sell across Etsy, PayPal and POD sites get 1099-K forms that report gross rather than net amounts, sometimes duplicated, under thresholds that kept changing, and the confusion lands at tax time.
- **Online marketplaces (Etsy)** (creator-tools, opp. 29): Shops are suspended permanently without warning or a stated reason, and appeals go unanswered or are denied with no explanation, cutting off an artist's income overnight.
- **Release planning / distribution QC** (creator-tools, opp. 29): Releases get stuck in processing or bounced late for cover art rules (a pictured LP, a store logo, 'too racy'), so artists miss announced release dates and the 7-day editorial pitch window.
- **Washer & dryer chimes** (laundry, opp. 26): The end-of-cycle chime is very loud by default, is silenced with hidden button combinations that differ by model, and some beeps can't be turned off at all
- **Gas lawn mowers** (outdoor, opp. 23): After winter storage the mower won't start, because ethanol fuel left in the carburetor goes stale and gums it up
- **Taxes / 1099s** (creator-tools, opp. 23): At tax time, musicians get overlapping 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC and PayPal 1099-K forms for the same royalty and sales income (Bandcamp warns about this), and international artists are confused about W-8BEN withholding.
- **Bedside clocks & chargers** (bedroom, opp. 19): Clock displays and charging lights are too bright for a dark room, and dimming offers only two or three still-bright steps, so people tape over them

### Mapping mismatch (6)

- **Portfolio sites and social platforms (Squarespace, Meta)** (creator-tools, opp. 51): Artists can't tell whether their portfolio is protected from AI training: Squarespace turned AI crawler access on by default and its toggle covers only a few named crawlers, while Meta gives most non-EU users no opt-out at all.
- **Shower valves & controls** (bathroom, opp. 38): Every shower works differently; people can't tell which way is hot, how to switch tub to shower, or where the control is
- **Music distributors / DSP metadata** (creator-tools, opp. 38): Releases land on another artist's Spotify/Apple profile with the same name (or a duplicate profile is created), and artists get passed between the distributor, which may only offer a takedown, and DSP support.
- **Royalty splits / producer collaboration** (creator-tools, opp. 30): Distributor splits cover only master income from that one distributor, collaborators must accept an invite (or the owner pays $10/yr for them) before earnings are released, and publishing splits stay on informal split sheets, so disputes freeze PRO payouts.
- **Stovetop controls** (kitchen, opp. 21): A row of knobs controls a 2×2 grid of burners, so people turn on the wrong burner
- **PROs (live performance royalties)** (creator-tools, opp. 14): Performing songwriters lose live royalties because each PRO needs setlists entered by hand in its own portal (BMI Live, ASCAP OnStage) within a limited window, and pay depends on whether the venue is licensed.
