Ranked complaints

    How scores work. Each complaint is scored 1–5 on frequency, harm (severity), annoyance, how hard it is to work around, and how buildable the fix is. Pain = frequency × the higher of harm and annoyance × workaround, scaled to 0–100, so a daily nuisance counts as much as real harm. Opportunity = pain × feasibility ÷ 5. Scores are judgement calls made from the linked sources, not review counts.
