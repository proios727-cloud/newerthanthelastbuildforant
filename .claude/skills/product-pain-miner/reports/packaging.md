**Opportunity** = pain × feasibility. **Pain** = frequency × max(severity, annoyance) × workaround difficulty (0–100). A nuisance counts as much as harm.

| 1 | Online-order shipping boxes | Tiny items arrive in huge boxes stuffed with filler, wasting cardboard and space for recycling | Seller-first | 51 | **41** | Boxes cut to the item's size at packing, or padded mailers for small items, and items shipped in their own retail box when it's sturdy enough |
| 2 | Chip bags & snack boxes | Bags and boxes are half empty, so buyers feel they're paying for air, especially after shrinkflation cuts | Seller-first | 51 | **41** | Packages sized to the contents, a printed 'contents settle to here' fill line or a clear window, and the price per ounce printed on the front |
| 3 | Clamshell blister packs | Sealed plastic clamshells need scissors or a knife to open, and opening them sends thousands of people a year to the emergency room with cuts | Seller-first | 48 | **38** | A paperboard sleeve with a pull-strip perforation, with anti-theft moved to a peg tag or electronic security tag instead of welded plastic |
| 4 | Child-resistant pill bottles | Push-and-twist caps are painful or impossible for people with arthritis, leading to missed doses or bottles left open | One size fits all | 38 | **30** | Keep child resistance but change how it's beaten: a two-button squeeze spaced for adult-length fingers, with a large textured grip, instead of grip strength plus a twist |
| 5 | Recycling labels | Recycling symbols are misread (the Green Dot is taken to mean recyclable), so many people guess or put packaging in the general waste | No feedback | 29 | **29** | A plain-words label for each part, such as 'Box: recycle · Film: general waste · Cap: leave on', in place of codes and look-alike logos |
| 6 | Cereal box liners | The inner bag rips unevenly down the side when opened, spills cereal into the box, and can't be reclosed | Seller-first | 26 | **21** | A liner with a laser-scored tear line across the top and a press-to-close zipper, with the box flap shaped to hold it shut |
