Norwegian Prima

The first ship of NCL's Prima class, and the line's clearest attempt at a resort-grade vessel.

Gross tonnage
143,535 GT
Guests
~3,100
Interiors
Studio Dado
Ocean Boulevard
44,000 sq ft

Studio Dado designed a more elegant, residential ship than NCL has sailed before: a vertical bow, midship engines, and Ocean Boulevard, an outdoor promenade that wraps the ship. Critics praise the architecture and the finish. Cruisers who have sailed her mention smaller venues, steady reservation pressure, and a list of paid attractions.

Book it if

You care about design, you travel as a couple, and a few upcharges won't spoil the week.

Skip it if

You want a big free pool deck, or you hate managing reservations.

Sea day survival & deck flow

Less pool deck. More places to hide.

Prima spreads people across many smaller venues. That works well until everyone wants the same one.

Where everyone goes

Ocean Boulevard and Infinity Beach are the main outdoor spaces. The main pool deck is smaller than on older NCL ships.

Pinch points

Smaller venues fill first and stay full. Expect queues and scarce loungers at the popular spots by late morning.

Escape zones

The quieter stretches of Ocean Boulevard later in the day. The Haven, if your cabin gives you access.
