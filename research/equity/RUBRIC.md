# Scoring rubric (draft — pending approval)

## Hard gates (fail any = excluded, not scored)
1. Verifier status PASS: no material unsupported claim.
2. Ticker/CIK/company identity confirmed against SEC EDGAR.
3. Latest 10-K and 10-Q are on file and current (no late-filing notice, NT 10-K/Q).
4. No going-concern language or auditor resignation in the last 12 months.
5. At least one dated catalyst within 180 days, from a primary source.
6. Bear analysis completed before the bull thesis was shown to it.
7. Average daily dollar volume ≥ $20M (S&P 500 constituents normally pass).

## Weighted score (0–100)
| Component | Weight | 5 = best | 1 = worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mispricing evidence | 25 | Specific, verified gap between filings and consensus framing | Narrative only |
| Business quality | 20 | Rising ROIC, FCF conversion > 80%, stable margins | Deteriorating |
| Valuation vs history and peers | 20 | Bottom quartile of 10y range and peer set, explainable | Top quartile |
| Catalyst clarity | 15 | Confirmed date ≤ 90 days, measurable | Unconfirmed / vague |
| Balance sheet | 10 | Net cash or net debt/EBITDA < 1.5x | > 4x or near-term maturity wall |
| Bear-case severity (inverted) | 10 | Bear risks bounded and observable | Thesis-breaking risk with no early signal |

Each component is scored 1–5 and multiplied by weight/5. Evidence strength scales the total: every material claim
labeled ESTIMATE or SPECULATION subtracts 3 points (max −15).

## Selection
Rank qualified candidates; publish the top 5–10 only. If fewer than 5 pass, publish fewer and say why.
