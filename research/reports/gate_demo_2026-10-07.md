# Five gates, one honest verdict: how I filter fake Sharpe ratios

I ran my desk's deployability gate on a real backtest today — an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck
mean-reversion strategy on the XOM/CVX spread. Five years, both legs costed,
delay-1 fills. On paper: +5.1% total, 4.5% max drawdown, "market-neutral."

It failed 13 checks. It will never touch capital.

## The five gates (every strategy must clear all of them)

**Gate 1 — Are the settings honest?**
Fees ON, slippage at or above a floor, no frictionless fairy tales. This backtest paid 10bps/side on both legs.

**Gate 2 — Is the edge real?**
- 100+ closed trades minimum. This one had **24**. A Sharpe computed on 24 trades is noise wearing a suit.
- Sharpe > 1 AND profit factor > 1.3. This one: **0.39**.
- Positive expectancy per trade.
- Drawdown within budget (this passed — 4.5%).
- Beats buy-and-hold. This one returned **+5.1% while XOM/CVX drift returned ~+58%**. You took 10x the effort to capture 1/10th of the market.
- Beta near zero for long/short books. Unverifiable here — 0.84 beta is not a hedge, it's a long position with extra steps.

**Gate 3 — The luck check (my favorite)**
If you tested 8 variants, the best one is expected to show a Sharpe of ~0.91 **by pure chance**.
This strategy's 0.39 Sharpe doesn't even clear the luck bar. Most "alpha" you see posted
online is exactly this: the best of N coin flips, presented as skill.
Rule: your Sharpe must beat the best-of-N-by-chance threshold, where N = every variant you tried.

**Gate 4 — Is it fragile?**
- Must survive 2x costs
- Neighbor parameters must keep most of the Sharpe (plateau, not a spike)
- Last 1 year must not be negative

**Gate 5 — Forward proof**
Paper trade it. Days of live tracking at a Sharpe consistent with the backtest before anything is real.

## The verdict that matters

The five-gate check is one file. It reads a JSON report and exits 0 (deployable)
or 1 (blocked). No vibes, no "looks promising," no survivorship theater.

The strategy failed: too few trades, Sharpe below the luck bar, massively underperforming
its own benchmark, no stress tests, no paper track.

**A blocked strategy is a win.** The money you don't lose to overfit
is identical to the money you make.
